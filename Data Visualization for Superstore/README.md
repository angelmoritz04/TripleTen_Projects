# Data Visualizations for Superstore

## Overview
<p>A grocery chain "Superstore" is in need of some serious recon. In this project, I created advanced visualizations with Tableau to determine profits and losses within subcategories, pinpointed the 3 best months of the year in which to do advertising, and tracked products with an abnormally high rate of return.</p>

## Features and Functionality
- Several different types of charts (bar, histogram, line, and heatmaps) to identify where the company is losing money
- Data is sorted in order, so, for example, the highest-returning users can be easily identified.
- A scatterplot where circles are darker if the item has high returns, and bigger if it has high sales. This allows the examiner to see where popular items might actually be loss-leaders.

## Findings of the Analysis
- The product OFF-BI-10004995 (also a type of binder) shipped through Standard Class has lost our company over $24,000
- The highest-performing states throughout the year are New York, California, and Washington, while the most profitable months are September, March, and December.
- The company should invest more in copiers, because they have a high profit and low rate of return.
