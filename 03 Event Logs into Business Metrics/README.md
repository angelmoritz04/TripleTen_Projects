# Event Logs into Business Metrics

## Overview
<p> This project was an exploration of conversion funnels and user metrics. Using Google Sheets, I created visuals to track turnover from each stage of the purchasing process. I also analyzed monthly cohorts, to determine whether users were returning to the site after an initial purchase.</p>

## Features and Functionality
- Cleaned and expanded raw data first_purchase_month and cohort_age
- Created several pivot tables determine how users were transitioning from "pageview," to "add_to_cart", to "checkout," to "purchase."
- Created pivot tables to track cohorts

## Findings of the Analysis
- Users stay relatively engaged throughout the process of using the page, with 29% of users adding items to carts. A greater success happens within the conversion from shopping cart to purchase, as 35% of people with shopping carts make a purchase
- Retention is low and only continues to drop throughout cohorts. By the 2nd month and 4th cohort age, only 0.5% of users returned to make another purchase.
- The site does not hold onto users well. While the conversion between cohorts is high, keeping customers coming back from each cohort age has been a struggle. It may be time to examine strategies for reminding customers about the business, encouraging them to return to us for electronics' instead of our competitors.
